A visual graph of Wikipedia articles. The edges of the graph are weighted by a link-based similarity measure developed by Milne and Witten (2008).[1]

    Distances between themes are enlarged for clarity; within a theme, positions are unchanged.

    Closeness to the war

    rank 1

    The star is the Hundred Years' War article. Each wedge is a theme, and the closer an article sits to the center, the more related it is to the war.

    Each article sits near the articles it's most related to, so themes form regions. Lines join each article to its 10 most related.

    Darker dots are closer to the war. Hover an article for its name, click it for details.
